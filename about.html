Our Founder

Jared Krickenbarger, HT(ASCP)

Founder & CEO · Certified Histology Technician

Jared is a certified histologist who has been a Mohs consultant since 2014 and founded Mohs Plus in 2019.

After more than a decade of hands-on experience in Mohs histology, Jared has trained dozens of techs, set up labs across the country, and helped clinics from solo practices to major health systems raise the quality and speed of their tissue processing.

His approach is collaborative and adaptive — meeting each tech and each lab where they are, and leaving them measurably better than he found them.
