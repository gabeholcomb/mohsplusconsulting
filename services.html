Services

A full range of services to keep your lab running.

At Mohs Plus® we offer a full range of services to help you get your lab optimized and running smoothly. You can trust the experience and training of Mohs Plus®.

01.
Service One

Mohs Tech Training

Mohs Plus® provides expert, hands-on training for enhancing the skill sets of all Mohs lab techs.

Our goal is to help Mohs techs master their trade, and become efficient and dependable assets. We customize our training to fit the needs of each individual — meeting your team where they are and bringing them up to a standard you can rely on.

  • On-site, hands-on instruction
  • Customized to each technician
  • Frozen section slide preparation
  • Tissue processing efficiency
  • Quality control protocols
  • Ongoing support and review
02.
Service Two

Mohs Tech Coverage

When your tech is out, your practice can't be. We've got you covered.

If in the event your tech falls ill, goes on maternity leave or resigns, we offer a multitude of options to support you during this transition. Long-term, short-term, and emergency coverage are all available — we travel directly to your practice and step in seamlessly, so your patients are never left waiting.

  • Long-term coverage
  • Short-term relief
  • Emergency response
  • Maternity & medical leave
  • Vacation coverage
  • Nationwide travel
03.
Service Three

Mohs Lab Setup

Starting a Mohs lab? We'll help you build it right the first time.

If you are interested in starting a Mohs Lab, we will provide a free over-the-phone consultation to help get you started. We can support you on this journey by assisting with laboratory design, equipment placement, manual and log creation, and the CLIA application process and review. We are committed to ensuring this process gives you a head start on your path to providing Mohs surgery services.

  • Free phone consultation
  • Laboratory design
  • Equipment placement
  • Manual & log creation
  • CLIA application support
  • Ongoing review & guidance
